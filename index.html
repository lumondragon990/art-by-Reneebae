About the artist

Renee Aliza Martinez — better known as Reneebae — is a Houston-based artist working in acrylic, collage and mixed media. Her work pulls from graffiti walls, vintage magazines, cartoons and outer space, stitched together into pieces that feel nostalgic and brand new at the same time.

Follow along on Instagram @rayrayreneebae for new work, process videos and drops.
