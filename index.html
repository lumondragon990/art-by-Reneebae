Meet Reneebae

Renee Aliza Martinez — Reneebae to everyone who knows her — is a Houston-based multidisciplinary artist working in acrylic, collage, spray paint and mixed media. Her work pulls from graffiti walls, vintage magazines, cartoons and outer space, cut up and stitched back together into pieces that feel nostalgic and brand new at the same time.

Nothing here comes off a production line. Every canvas is painted, torn, layered and sealed by hand in her studio — and when it sells, it's gone. She also hand-paints wearable pieces: one-of-one tees, jackets and bags that turn her canvases into streetwear.

Collectors, first-time buyers, and anyone who wants something nobody else owns: this is your stop. Follow the work in progress on Instagram @rayrayreneebae.
